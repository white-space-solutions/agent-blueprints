# The agent job card

The one-page worksheet from the Family Mastermind breakout: the six questions
to answer before building any agent. Fill it in for one job in your business
and you have the brief for a build.

_The PDF version is what was handed out; the text below is a stub until the
final wording is published._

1. **Role.** Who is this agent, in one sentence a new hire would understand?
2. **Responsibilities.** What is it accountable for, and what number says it did the job?
3. **Skills.** What does it need to know how to do, as written instructions it can follow?
4. **Tools.** What systems does it read, and which does it write to? Writes need an approval gate.
5. **Knowledge.** What documents does it work from, who owns them, and how do they change?
6. **Guardrails.** What must it never do, whatever the lead says?
