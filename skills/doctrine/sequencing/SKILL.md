# Deciding what to run

Every lead sits in exactly one state. Find the state, then run that state's rule set from
the Lead Management Game Plan. Do not improvise a cadence.

## Finding the state

Ask in this order and stop at the first yes:

Live uncancelled Cal.com booking? → Sequence 2, Pre-Booking. This outranks every CRM flag,
including Disqualified. Confirm and prime, always.
Booking missed or cancelled in the last 14 days? → Sequence 5, Re-Booking.
Call held, not closed, no proposal out? → Sequence 6, Post-Call Nurture.
Proposal sent? → Sequence 8, Proposal Follow-Through. Alex owns the deal, you own the rhythm.
Brand new, first touch not yet sent? → Sequence 1, Intake, then first touch inside 5 minutes.
Interested and a clear ICP fit, no booking? → Sequence 3, Get-Booked.
Fit or interest unclear? → Sequence 4, Exploration.
Exhausted a sequence without converting, or gone quiet 30+ days? → Sequence 7, Long-Term Nurture.

## The cadences

Pre-Booking: daily from booking through the morning of. Email with the brand VSL, plus SMS
at booking, day before, and 60 minutes out.
Get-Booked: day 0, 1, 2, 4, 7, 10, 14, 21, 30. Alternate email and SMS. Never both on one
day unless Alex flagged the lead hot.
Exploration: day 0 email, day 2 SMS, day 5 email, day 10 email, then monthly. Resolve
within 30 days.
Re-Booking: within 5 minutes of the miss, then day 2 if no reply. Two no-shows ends it.
Post-Call Nurture: weekly if pending a specific answer, monthly if timing-based or cool.
Proposal Follow-Through: day 2, 5, 10, 21.
Long-Term Nurture: every 2 to 3 days for weeks 0 to 3, weekly weeks 4 to 6, biweekly then
monthly through month 12 and beyond.

## The one rule that governs all of them

Every touch adds something: a new angle, a proof point, a useful observation, an answer to
an open question. Never a bare bump. If you have nothing new for a scheduled touch, skip
it and note why. A skipped touch costs nothing. A filler touch costs the relationship.

## Stop conditions

Any opt-out signal, however informal. "Not interested", "wrong person", "remove me", STOP.
Two no-shows.
Silence after a breakup note, which routes to Sequence 7 rather than to nothing.
status_label of Customer, Bad Fit or Not Interested, or Qualified of Disqualified, EXCEPT
where a live booking overrides.
Referrals, which Alex handles unless he delegates one.

## Channel choice per touch

Email when the touch carries depth: proof points, numbers, the VSL, an answer with
substance.
SMS when the touch is a nudge, a confirmation, or a same-day reminder.
Both only when a hot lead is due a push and the two genuinely differ. Never a text that
restates the email.
