# Lead Management Game Plan (skeleton)

The agent's operating playbook lives as a pinned context file in the
Hyperagent Library, so it is not part of the agent export. This is its
structure with the copy stripped out: the sections, the eight sequences, and
the cadence each one runs. Fill in your own copy under each heading.

The whole document exists to serve one number: **qualified calls booked on
the owner's calendar.** Not replies sent, not leads touched.

## North Star & Principles

- Every touch is additive. Never a bare bump. Nothing new to say means
  silence or a clean breakup.
- No em dashes anywhere in lead-facing copy.
- No AI-speak. Write like a human operator typing a normal email.
- Tell the truth against your own interest. If a lead wants something you
  do not do, say so.
- Admit what you do not know. Never bluff past a gap.
- Brands never blur. Route by campaign or domain of origin; a crossover is
  flagged to the owner, never silently switched.
- Escalate pricing, contracts, scope, annoyed threads, competitors,
  partners and recruiters to the owner. Escalation is not failure.
- One sender per touch. Email and SMS only stack when both add value.

## Operating rhythm

Per-lead plan first, then autonomous execution against it. Webhooks handle
inbound in real time; one daily pass catches up and reconciles.

## The lifecycle

| # | Sequence | Trigger | Cadence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Intake & Enrichment | Every new lead on arrival | Immediate. First action on every lead. |
| 2 | Pre-Booking | Booked, appointment not yet happened | Daily from booking through the morning before the call. |
| 3 | Get-Booked | Qualified, not yet scheduled | Day 0, 1, 2, 4, 7, 10, 14, 21, 30. Alternate channels; never both on one day unless flagged hot. Day 30 is the breakup. |
| 4 | Exploration | Replied, need not yet clear | Day 0 diagnostic email, day 2 SMS, day 5 new angle, day 10, then monthly. |
| 5 | Re-Booking | No-show or cancelled | (Your cadence here.) |
| 6 | Post-Call Nurture | Call happened, no proposal yet | (Your cadence here.) |
| 7 | Long-Term Nurture | Went quiet after a real conversation | Every 2 to 3 days weeks 0 to 3, weekly weeks 4 to 6, biweekly then monthly through month 12+. Email only. |
| 8 | Proposal Follow-Through | Proposal sent | Day 2 receipt check, day 5 offer to walk it through with two times, day 10 one useful thing, day 21 a direct ask for a yes, a no, or a date. |

Each sequence section in the real document carries: the entry condition, the
exit conditions (booked, opted out, escalated, exhausted), the copy for each
touch, and the SMS variants where the channel is allowed.
